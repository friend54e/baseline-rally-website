Why Sponsor Baseline Rally?

Sponsoring Baseline Rally (BLR) allows your business to connect with an active and growing local automotive community. While each event may reach a more focused group in person, the professional photography, video coverage, attendee content, and continued online promotion generated from our rallies extend far beyond the event itself.

Content from BLR events can generate thousands of views across Instagram, Facebook, and other social platforms, giving sponsors continued exposure before, during, and after each rally. Through branded merchandise, vehicle decals, social media features, event photography, videography, and on-site partnerships, your business can become directly associated with the local automotive community and the memorable experiences BLR creates.

Baseline Rally operates with little to no profit in order to keep our events affordable and accessible. Because of this, we depend heavily on sponsors who share our passion for cars and community. Sponsor contributions help us cover event costs, improve the participant experience, and continue creating well-organized rallies without placing a major financial burden on attendees.

We aim to build genuine partnerships with businesses that believe in supporting automotive culture. By sponsoring BLR, your business helps us continue bringing enthusiasts together while becoming part of a community built around a shared love of cars.
