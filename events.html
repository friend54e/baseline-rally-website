Saturday, August 22, 2026  ·  9:30 – 11:30 a.m.

BLR × MC3 Fall Rally Kickoff — Cars & Coffee

Join us for a morning filled with great cars, coffee from Motor City Car Cafe, beverages provided by Red Bull, and the opportunity to connect with the BLR and MC3 communities.

This event will also serve as the official kickoff for the announcement of our 2026 Fall Rally. Guests who attend in person will receive exclusive early access to purchase rally tickets one day before they are released to the public. Public ticket sales will officially open on Sunday, August 23.

Come check out the MC3 facility, meet the BLR crew, and be among the first to learn more about our upcoming rally.
